eScorecard Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

eScorecard is a golf scorecard app by Alan Stewart. This page explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

What stays on your device

Your rounds, scorecards, courses, players and handicaps are stored only on your device. We do not collect them, and they are not sent anywhere unless you choose to share or export them yourself.

Competitions

If you create or join a competition and submit a finished card, the app sends the following to our server:

  • the names of the players on the card;
  • each player's gross strokes on every hole, the stroke index of each hole, and the Stableford points scored on each hole;
  • the name of the course;
  • a random anonymous identifier created for your installation of the app. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or any account, and you are never asked to sign up.

Nothing is sent unless you tap Submit result. Creating or joining a competition sends the competition's name, date, format and code.

Why we use it

Only to run the competition: to show a ranked results table to the organiser and the players who have the competition's code, in the app and on the web page. The anonymous identifier lets your device change or remove the entries it sent, and lets the organiser remove entries from their own competition.

Who can see it

Anyone who has a competition's code can view that competition's results, including the player names and scores on each card. Only submit names you have permission to share.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

The app does not track you, show advertising, or use analytics or third-party advertising services. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Where it is stored

Competition data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Data is sent over encrypted connections. The anonymous sign-in token is kept in your device's keychain.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Competition data is kept until it is deleted. The organiser can delete a competition and all its results in the app, and can remove individual entries. The device that sent an entry can also remove it. To ask for your information to be deleted, email stewartie@yahoo.co.uk with the competition code and the names to remove.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them beyond the player names a user types in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
